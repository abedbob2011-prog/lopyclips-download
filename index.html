FAQ

Questions clippers ask.

Does it post for me?

No. You stay in control of your accounts. Every Short comes with its title, caption and the steps to post it on YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Instagram, and the finished video is saved to a folder.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 with 8 GB of RAM and about 10 GB of free space. No graphics card is needed. A Mac version is planned.

Can I share my copy with a friend?

Each licence key works on one PC at a time. You can move it to a new PC from the app's settings.

Will I make money?

That depends on the campaigns you join and how your clips perform. Clipster saves you the hours of finding and editing moments; it cannot guarantee views or payouts.

Privacy policy

What Clipster does with your data

  • Your videos, clips, settings and channel list stay on your own PC.
  • To check your licence, the app sends your licence key and a random ID for your PC to our licence server. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • When you use the AI helper or the moment finder with the included AI, the text of the transcript or your question is sent to our AI service to get an answer. It is not stored or used for anything else.
  • If you connect a YouTube account, the sign-in token is stored only on your PC and is used only to upload videos you approve and to read back their status.
  • We never sell your data and use no advertising trackers.
  • To delete everything, uninstall the app and delete its data folder.
